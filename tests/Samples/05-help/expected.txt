Help (aggdef)

'aggdef' command
    Find out where to set your aggdef slider to be 1/1
    !aggdef <attack time> <recharge time> <init value>

If you are lazy, just use
    !weapon <drop weapon into chat>

Example:
    Your weapon has an attack time of 1.2 seconds and a recharge time of 1.5 seconds.
    Your melee/ranged init is 1200:
    /tell Nadybot !aggdef 1.2 1.5 1200

See !help syntax for an explanation of the command syntax
